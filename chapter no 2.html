Chapter 2

Zimal suddenly opened her eyes and sat up on the bed. "Gosh… again?" she whispered tiredly. It was the same dream. The same fear. The same unknown feeling haunting her every night. She rubbed her face and quickly got ready for work. Zimal worked at one of the biggest development companies in the country, a company famous for land development and huge building projects. The company was powerful, respected, and feared by many people. Getting a job there was not easy, but somehow Zimal had gotten the opportunity by pure luck. After getting ready, she left for the office. On the way, she met her best friend Afifa, who also worked in the same company. Afifa immediately noticed Zimal's tired face. "Don't tell me…" Afifa sighed dramatically. "You saw that weird dream again?" Zimal looked at her quietly. "Yes… again." Afifa rolled her eyes. "Please stop thinking about it. I'm in a really good mood today, and I don't want your horror dreams to ruin it." Zimal smirked slightly. "Let me guess… someone made a place in your heart again?" Afifa gasped dramatically. "How do you always catch me so quickly?" Zimal laughed softly. "Because you fall in love every two business days." Afifa ignored her teasing and continued excitedly, "This time he's different. He's rich, handsome, tall, innocent-looking, and—" "Enough," Zimal interrupted while laughing. "This is nothing new. We've reached the office, so stop saying nonsense." Afifa crossed her arms playfully. "Okay, madam." Both of them entered the company building. The atmosphere inside was serious as usual. Employees rushed from one department to another while everyone remained careful with their work. Zimal quietly sat at her desk and continued the unfinished work from yesterday. After some time, the manager, Ibrahim, entered the department with a new employee beside him. "Miss Zimal," Ibrahim said professionally, "you'll guide the new employee and explain the company rules to him." "Yes, sir," Zimal replied politely. "Good. Also, everyone needs to gather in the CEO's office in ten minutes." After saying that, Ibrahim left. The moment his words echoed through the department, the atmosphere suddenly changed. Everyone became nervous. Some employees quickly fixed their files while others straightened themselves in fear. The new employee looked confused. "Miss Zimal," he asked softly, "why is everyone acting like this? I think I saw the CEO once. He doesn't look that scary… actually, he looks quite handsome." Zimal looked at him for a moment before speaking quietly. "Mr. Sahriar Sikander is not just a CEO… everyone in this company fears him." The employee looked surprised. "You're new here, right?" Zimal smiled nervously. "Then remember one thing carefully… never make Sahriar Sikander angry." She lowered her voice slightly. "A single mistake can cost you your job… especially if it happens in front of him." The employee swallowed nervously while listening. "He may look calm," Zimal continued softly, "but Sahriar Sikander is the kind of man no one dares to challenge." She looked around carefully before speaking again. "In this company, his words are treated like orders. Nobody dares to ignore him." The employee listened silently while Zimal explained further. "He controls this entire empire with a single decision. That's the kind of power Sahriar Sikander has." A faint smile appeared on her face. "One signature from him can build someone's career… or destroy it completely." The employee became even more nervous. "People don't fear only his anger," Zimal whispered. "They fear his power." She looked toward the large glass office at the end of the hallway. "This company runs on Mr. Sahriar Sikander's rules… and nobody dares to break them." The new employee finally introduced himself. "My name is Zawar," he said politely. Zimal nodded. "People fear his name, respect his power, and envy his success," she continued. "Sahriar Sikander is the youngest and most ruthless CEO in the country." She paused before speaking again. "His father, Chairman Sikander Hayat, owns multiple companies around the world. But this company… and many businesses in the UK, London, and America… are personally controlled by Mr. Sahriar Sikander." Zawar looked shocked. "But despite all that success," Zimal added quietly, "he's rude, arrogant, strict, and always in a bad mood. Sometimes it feels like he thinks the entire world belongs to him." Afifa suddenly joined the conversation dramatically. "So stay away from him if you want peace in your life." Zimal laughed softly. "Exactly." Zawar nodded carefully. "Understood, Miss Zimal." A few moments later, everyone gathered inside the CEO's office for the meeting. The atmosphere became silent immediately after Sahriar Sikander entered the room. His cold eyes scanned everyone once before he sat down confidently. Nobody dared to speak unnecessarily. The meeting started professionally and continued for almost an hour. Even during the meeting, everyone remained careful with every word they spoke. After it finally ended, the employees quietly returned to their cabins. Zimal spent some time explaining the office rules and work system to Zawar before finishing her own tasks. By evening, she finally returned home. Her house was beautiful and peaceful, but the most beautiful place for her was her room. Unlike the rest of the house, Zimal had decorated her room exactly the way she wanted. It looked like a small princess room filled with soft lights, books, flowers, and warm colors. After dinner, she quietly went upstairs. She was never very close to her family. Her family was small — her father, mother, elder brother Zakaria, and herself — yet Zimal always preferred staying alone. Without speaking much, she lay down on her bed, exhausted from the day. Just as she was about to sleep, her phone suddenly rang. It was Afifa. Zimal answered lazily. "What now?" Afifa sounded unusually excited. "Someone is literally dying to get your number. Should I give it to him?" Zimal frowned immediately. "Afifa, please stop your nonsense." "I'm serious this time." "Who even wants my number?" Afifa laughed mysteriously. "If I tell you the name, you'll be shocked." Zimal rolled her eyes. "I'm not interested." "Really?" Afifa teased. "Not even a little curious?" "No," Zimal replied stubbornly while lying back on her bed. "And thanks, but I don't want my number to be shared with random people." Afifa laughed loudly from the other side of the call. "Fine… but don't regret it later." Zimal ended the call and stared silently at the ceiling. For some unknown reason… Her heart suddenly felt uneasy again.
